Distance to target
--
Eve / phone height (m)
Line the crosshair up with the base of the object where it meets the ground, keep your phone tilted down, and hold steady. Distance is estimated as height ÷ tan(aim angle) — approximate, works best outdoors with GPS accuracy reachable.
Distance to object
--
Object height (m)
Align the crosshair with the BASE of the object and tap “Mark base”, then align with the TOP and tap “Mark top”. Enter the object's real height (a standard door is about 2 m).
Person's height
--
Phone height (m)
Aim the crosshair at the person's FEET on the ground and tap “Mark feet” — this finds the distance to them (phone height ÷ tan angle). Then aim at the very TOP of their head and tap “Mark head” to compute their height.
